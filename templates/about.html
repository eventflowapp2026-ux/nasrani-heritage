Our Mission

"This platform exists to preserve, explain, and pass on the heritage, liturgy, language, and traditions of the Nasrani community to the next generation."

Who We Are

The Nasrani (St. Thomas Christian) community traces its origins to the apostolic ministry of St. Thomas the Apostle, who arrived in Kerala, India in 52 AD. For nearly 2000 years, we have preserved our unique identity, blending East Syriac traditions with Indian culture.

This platform is dedicated to documenting, discussing, and preserving this rich heritage for future generations. From ancient manuscripts to modern interpretations, from liturgical traditions to cultural practices — we aim to create a comprehensive digital archive of Nasrani heritage.

What We Offer

  • Historical articles
  • Liturgy explanations
  • Syriac language resources
  • Chant recordings
  • Community discussions
  • Feast traditions
  • Research papers
  • Manuscript archive
