Community Guidelines

Respecting our heritage, respecting each other

Our Core Values

  • Respect: Honor the diversity of opinions and interpretations within our community
  • Accuracy: Strive for historical and liturgical accuracy in all discussions
  • Inclusivity: Welcome all who seek to learn about Nasrani heritage
  • Preservation: Focus on documenting and preserving, not distorting, our traditions

Do's

  • ✓ Cite sources for historical claims
  • ✓ Be respectful when discussing different traditions
  • ✓ Ask questions if you're unsure about something
  • ✓ Share resources and knowledge generously
  • ✓ Report content that violates guidelines

Don'ts

  • ✗ No personal attacks or harassment
  • ✗ No hate speech or discrimination
  • ✗ No spam or self-promotion
  • ✗ No deliberate misinformation
  • ✗ No commercial advertising
  • ✗ No disrespect to religious sentiments

Moderation

Our moderation team reviews reported content and takes appropriate action, which may include:

  • Warning users about inappropriate behavior
  • Editing or removing content that violates guidelines
  • Temporary or permanent suspension of accounts
  • Banning users who repeatedly violate guidelines
Questions about these guidelines? Contact our moderation team at moderators@nasraniheritage.org
